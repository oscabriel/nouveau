# Gate 3 shipped as Save only

Extended by ADR-0016 (2026-09-20): saves became the try list on `/$user`, with the log-removes-save rule and its undo. Coffee watches, dated and private logs and in-app updates stay deferred.

The revised product spec (`.agents/research/nouveau-reimagined-product-spec.md`, Gate 3, and issue #20) asked for saved coffees, coffee-specific watches, dated logs, a log audience rule, in-app updates independent of email, and a My coffee navigation. With a week to the 2026-09-22 deadline and the video, testers, and post still unstarted, we shipped Save alone (`0522e79`) and deferred the rest. The reasoning: Save is the one step of the §11 demo sequence that did not exist, and it is self-contained. Log privacy is all-or-nothing across every public query, lot page, profile, and activity result, and a half-finished audience model is worse than a clearly labelled "logs are public". Coffee watches would need a second watch relationship plus dedup against roaster watches in the alert fanout, none of which shows in a 2:40 video where "save it, then watch the roaster" already works. Dated logs and in-app updates change nothing a judge can see. Consequence: `PRODUCT.md` lists these under "Not in this release", #20 stays open as the post-hackathon list, and the demo says logs are public and saves are private.
