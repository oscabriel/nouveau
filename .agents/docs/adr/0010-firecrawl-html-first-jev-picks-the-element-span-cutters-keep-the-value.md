# Firecrawl's HTML is the page read's first source; Jev picks the element, the span cutters keep the value

Drafted 2026-09-19; accepted the same day (owner). Implementation follows in three pieces, reader first (see the handoff of 2026-09-19). Amends ADR-0008 (plain fetch first, Firecrawl markdown as the fallback) and the candidate-finding half of ADR-0005 (regex spans, Jev picks one); ADR-0009's reducer and alt lines stand. The question that started the review: can the page-facts path become "Firecrawl grabs the page, code splits it into elements, Jev classifies each element", replacing the regex tiers? Evidence: a live diff of one purchasable lot per seed roaster across four sources (plain fetch, Firecrawl `rawHtml`, `html` and `markdown`, 2026-09-19, `/tmp/nouveau-handoff/diff.md`), and a Jev probe of whole-line elements against the current span candidates on 19 of those pages (`jev-1.13.0`, `/tmp/nouveau-handoff/jev-probe.md`, raw answers in `probe.json`).

## Evidence

**Source.** Run through the repo's own reducer and finder (`pageTextFromHtml`, `pageFactCandidates`), plain HTML and Firecrawl's rendered `html` give the same candidates on 16 of 20 pages.

- Passenger's spec block is client-rendered (producer, "Origin: Mt. Kenya Region, Kenya"). The plain text is over `MIN_PAGE_TEXT_LENGTH`, so the shell check passes and the fallback never runs; only rendered HTML carries the block.
- Merit answers the plain fetch with 404 on the apex and www hosts, so every Merit read already falls back. Merit's and Ruby's feeds list unpublished handles (Merit's include an oven cleaner and tees); the feed-to-page mapping is its own problem, outside this record.
- Stumptown's extra "honey" came from a customer review widget in the rendered page. Intelligentsia's extra region was `&comma;` left undecoded (fixed 2026-09-19, the entity table now covers the names a census of all 20 pages found).
- Markdown is the worst source. It loses the producer on 5 and the region on 6 roasters (the label glues to the value), and adds nav and footer lines as note candidates on every page.
- Firecrawl's `html` text equals its `rawHtml` text on all 20 at about a third of the size. Rendered pages are 2 to 3 times the plain size; the extra words are reviews, chat, recaptcha, cart and menus, which the chrome and upsell cuts already drop.
- The Convex Firecrawl component (`@firecrawl/firecrawl-convex` 0.1.1) accepts `html` and `rawHtml` on `scrape` and returns both.

**Reader.** The probe compared A, the current pipeline (span candidates, one Choice per field, one Noul per note and sentence), with B, the proposal (every distinct line of the reduced text up to 160 characters is an option; one Choice per field over all lines; one Noul per line asking whether it lists the lot's tasting notes; the chosen line has a `Label:` prefix stripped, then `verifyPageFacts`). Same state for both, capped at `JEV_STATE_LIMIT`.

- No cap. Onyx sent 227 options per Choice and 233 questions in one request; Jev answered all of them in 304 ms. Every other page came back under 210 ms. A and B cost the same.
- Verified fields, A against B: process 14 and 14, roast level 5 and 5, variety 11 and 8, region 6 and 7, elevation 11 and 9, producer 7 and 15.
- B finds what the label router cannot. Regalia's spec block has no colons ("Process Washed", "Region Planadas, Tolima, Colombia", "Producer Jorge Rojas", "Altitud 2050 MASL"); A sees none of it, B's Choice picks the right line every time at 0.88 to 1.0. Merit's "Tasting notes: Prunes, Fig Danish, and Nutmeg" and Coava's "notes of red plum, brown sugar, & black tea" pass B's line Noul and never became A candidates. Counter Culture "Finca El Puente", Madcap "Irvin Izaguirre" and La Colombe "COOP/FARM : Lacador" are real producers B finds and A does not.
- A whole line is not a fact. Lot names verify as producers ("Kenya Karumandi" at 0.84, "Danche" at 0.92, "Rwanda - Rulindo Murambi - Washed" at 0.98); `verifyProducer` is shape-loose and confidence does not separate them. Without a colon the label stays inside the value ("Region Planadas, Tolima, Colombia"). A list fails as a whole: "Varietal: Typica, Pacas, Caturra, Catuaí, and San Salvador" (0.99) fails `verifyVariety`, so B loses four varieties A's span scan gets, and "Altitud 2050 MASL" fails the elevation shape.
- The verifier carries real load. Madcap's headline was picked for four fields and Coava's "available as Drip roast profile only" for roast, at 0.4 to 0.95; the verifier rejected every one.
- Notes need the shape filter. B's line Noul passes header lines ("NOTES", "Tasting Notes"), prose sentences, and "T" and "x" on Onyx alongside every real notes line.
- Two ambiguous lines: Eastpole producer, A "Smallholders" and B "Cipres" (the lot's name); Sweet Bloom process, A "washed" and B "washed, natural" from "washed & natural process", where B is right because the lot is a blend of two.

**Limits.** The prod Firecrawl key is on the Free plan: 10 scrapes a minute and 2 concurrent browsers, counted per team, with a 429 past either. `FIRECRAWL_FALLBACK_PER_MINUTE` was 60 and is 10 as of 2026-09-19. The crawler's own scrapes (collection pages, `product_pages` lots, bot-protected feeds) draw from the same 10 without passing through the bucket. A deferred read keeps its stamp and waits `PAGE_FACTS_RETRY_MS` (24 hours).

## Decision

- **Firecrawl first, `html` format.** `readPageFacts` asks Firecrawl for the page with `formats: ["html"]` and `maxAge: PAGE_FACTS_MAX_AGE_MS`, then runs the same reducer as today (`pageTextFromHtml`: main element, chrome and upsell cuts, theme notes hoist, labelled alt lines). Markdown is never read. `rawHtml` is not requested. The plain fetch stays as the fallback when Firecrawl answers 429 or errors, with `samePage` and the shell check unchanged, so a deferred read can still produce facts at no credit. Every read passes through the `firecrawlFallback` bucket, which is renamed to say what it now is: the deployment's Firecrawl read budget.
- **Reads are paced to the plan, not deferred for a day.** With Firecrawl first the bucket is the rule, not the exception, and the sweep's per-roaster spacing (`PAGE_SWEEP_SPACING_MS`) no longer bounds the deployment's rate: 20 roasters' sweeps at once put up to 500 reads into a 10-a-minute budget. A read the budget defers reschedules itself after the bucket's refill interval (the rate limiter reports `retryAfter`), keeps its stamp and its uncounted status, and gives up to the 24-hour window only after a bounded number of deferrals. On the Free plan a 500-lot backfill takes about 50 minutes; steady state after the backfill is a handful of reads an hour (the prod backfill of 729 reads finished 2026-09-19 and the hourly sweeps have scheduled nothing since).
- **Jev picks the element, code cuts the value, the verifier gates.** `pageFactCandidates` is replaced by a two-step reader:
  1. The reduced text is split into lines (the reducer's block text; the theme notes line and alt lines lead). Every distinct line up to `MAX_ELEMENT_LENGTH` (160 in the probe) is an option. One Choice per field over the lines plus the `none` hatch, each question naming the lot as ADR-0008 set. Notes: the note-shape filter first (separator present, no sentence punctuation, under `MAX_NOTE_LINE_LENGTH`), then one Noul per surviving line.
  2. The value is cut from the chosen line by the field's existing span cutter, never stored as the line: `findProcesses` (all terms, so "washed & natural" yields both), `roastFromValue`, `VARIETY_TERM` matches then `verifyVariety` on the joined list, `ELEVATION_SPAN` then `verifyElevation`, and for region and producer the label vocabulary stripped from the front (`PAGE_BARE_LABEL`'s word list, colon or no colon) then `verifyRegion` and `verifyProducer`. A note line goes through `splitNotes` and `verifyNotes`.
  3. `verifyPageFacts` runs last, unchanged in role: a country under region is an origin, "Espresso" is not a roast level, prose is not a fact.
- **Producer gains a lot-name guard.** A producer value equal to the lot's name, or contained in it after the origin and process words are removed, is dropped. `verifyProducer` stays a shape check; the guard lives in the page reader, which knows the name.
- **What the two-step retires.** `PAGE_LABEL_LINE`, `PAGE_LABEL_ONLY`, `PAGE_BARE_LABEL`'s router role, the next-line lookahead, `pushSpanCandidates`'s whole-page scans and `MAX_PAGE_CANDIDATES` (the probe sent 227 options without a cap and Jev answered). What stays: the reducer (ADR-0008, ADR-0009), the span cutters and shape verifiers in `lotFacts.ts` and `extraction.ts`, `verifyPageFacts`, `JEV_STATE_LIMIT` (which now also bounds the option list, since every option is a line of the state), the sweep and retry rules, `sentenceCandidates` for description sentences.
- **The feed's regex pile is a later phase, not this decision.** `parseLotAttributes` and `extractRoasterNotes` (about 650 lines over `body_html` and tags) could run through the same element-plus-Jev reader, which is the "one reader with two sources" the review named. The feed is re-extracted for every lot on every crawl (2,740 lots an hour), so that phase needs a change guard first: read with Jev only when a lot's description or tags differ from the last capture, keep the regex result otherwise. Not decided here; recorded so the direction survives.

## Why this and not the proposal as asked

- Jev cannot extract. It answers yes/no and picks one option; it never emits text. Every stored value is a string cut from the page, so the design choice is only the grain of the cut. The probe shows the element is the right grain for locating and the wrong grain for storing.
- Firecrawl first buys Passenger and Merit and loses nothing the plain fetch finds, at one credit per read, which the owner has said is not a concern. It does not raise coverage on the other 18 shops by itself: their facts are in served HTML already. The coverage gain comes from the reader, which is why both change together.
- Markdown stays rejected on the diff's numbers. The label-glued-to-value loss on 5 and 6 roasters is the exact failure the element pick needs to avoid.

## Consequences

- Passenger's spec block and Merit's page become readable. Regalia's colon-less block, Merit's and Coava's lead-in notes, and three real producers become facts. Expected: producer roughly doubles across the seed shops; process, roast level and elevation hold; variety and region hold once the cut runs on the chosen line.
- Every page read costs a credit and a slot in a 10-a-minute team budget shared with the crawler's own scrapes. Until the plan changes, a `product_pages` roaster's crawl and the sweep compete for the same 10. Raising the plan to Hobby (100 a minute, 5 concurrent) removes the pacing as a design concern; the ADR does not depend on it.
- The 12k state cap now bounds the options too: a spec block placed below a long story is outside both the state and the option list. The hoists (theme notes, alt lines) exist for this; a spec-block hoist by class name may follow if a roaster shows the failure.
- Upsell leakage stays guarded by the block cuts and the named question. Removing the block cuts is not part of this decision; the probe ran with them in place.
- Theme-embedded JSON (`script type="application/json"`) was not surveyed; ADR-0009 covered JSON-LD, `og:description` and the per-product JSON only. Open until one roaster's facts are found there and nowhere in the text.
- Tests. The label-router tests carry most of the 489-test suite for this area. They are retargeted at the cut step (line in, value out) with the 19 probe pages' reduced text as fixtures and `probe.json`'s answers as the stubbed Jev reply, so the reader is tested end to end without a network call. The router's own cases move to the cutter where the value survives and are deleted where the router was the only thing under test.
- `CONTEXT.md`, "Page facts": "Read verbatim from the page" holds; the source phrase becomes "the rendered page" once accepted. "Source mode" is unchanged (it describes the catalog read, not the page read).

## Amended 2026-09-19

The reader (piece 1 of the implementation plan) landed with the tests (piece 2) against the 19 probe pages, reduced through the repo's own reducer and paired with the recorded Jev answers (`convex/fixtures/probePages.json`). Where the implementation departs from the text above:

- **The producer guard tests for a country or a process term, not for the lot's name.** The rule as written (drop a producer equal to the lot's name, or contained in it) would have dropped Counter Culture "Finca El Puente", Madcap "Irvin Izaguirre" and PTS "Hacienda La Papaya", three of the producers the evidence counts as wins: a lot named after its producer is common. What separates the lot names that passed the shape ("Kenya Karumandi", "Rwanda - Rulindo Murambi - Washed") is that a producer's name carries no country and no process term. The guard needs no lot name, so `pageFactsFromPicks` takes only the picks. Heart's "Ethiopia Habtamu Fikadu" is lost to it; Sey's "Danche" and East Pole's "Cipres" (the two ambiguous picks) are kept.
- **The variety cut keeps the labelled list whole when it verifies, and falls back to the vocabulary terms when it does not.** The text said vocabulary terms only, which would have lost Regalia "Chiroso" and East Pole "Marsellesa" (neither in the vocabulary) while keeping PTS's five-term list as "Typica, Pacas, Caturra".
- **A line that leads with another field's label is never a note line.** "Altitude: 1,900 - 2,100 masl" has a separator and passed the shape filter, so it took a Noul and, had Jev passed it, would have stored "2" and "100 masl" as notes.
- **A popover, popup, tooltip or modal block is cut from the page like a dialog.** Counter Culture's flavor-wheel popover sits inside the notes wrapper, and the theme hoist read its copy as notes ("check out our", "Flavor Wheel"); Onyx's 23 hover popups were 60k of the 69k characters its page reduced to, so its spec block now fits the state without the hoists.
- Two shared shapes tightened: `NOTE_SEPARATOR` reads an Oxford ", &" as one separator (Coava's "brown sugar, & black tea" had left "& black tea" as a part), and "varied" and "various producers" join the placeholders no field stores.
- Confirmed on the fixtures: no pick lies past the state cap, so bounding the options by the state loses nothing on the seed shops. Facts across the 19 pages, feed-independent: process 14, notes 15, producer 10, elevation 10, variety 10, region 8, roast level 5.
- Review follow-ups (2c5fefe reviewed against 7b9980d): the label strip tolerates markdown emphasis and table rows, because Firecrawl's markdown stays the fallback until piece 3 ("**Region:** Huila", "| Producer | Finca Ojo de Agua |"); a label lead repeats only through a slash or ampersand ("Farmers Cooperative Kata Muduga" keeps "Cooperative Kata Muduga"); the page head ends on a whole line, so no option is a fragment; a page line that spells "none" is not an option.
- Piece 3 landed the same day (Firecrawl `html` first, paced deferrals). Where it departs from the Decision text:
  - **A deferred read reserves its slot instead of polling the bucket.** Rescheduling after `retryAfter` and asking again would have every read past the budget retry at each refill: twenty sweeps' 500 reads against 10 a minute is hundreds of thousands of action runs over the backfill. The rate limiter's `reserve` option hands the read the next free slot's time; the read reschedules itself once, for that time, and the rerun reads without taking another token (`scrape`'s `reserved` argument). The bucket is `firecrawlReads`, its rate `FIRECRAWL_READS_PER_MINUTE`.
  - **The bucket holds one token and its rate is one under the plan's.** The first prod run after the deploy (Passenger and Merit reset, 50 reads) hit Firecrawl's 429 in three consecutive minutes with the bucket at capacity 10: a token bucket admits its capacity plus its rate inside any fixed minute, and Firecrawl's minute is fixed. Worse, Firecrawl counts every request against the minute and the component retries a 429 three times with no option to turn it off, so one read over the line burned the rest of the minute ("Consumed (req/min): 14, 18, 22, 26, 30" in the logs). Capacity 1 at 9 a minute keeps any sixty seconds at 10 or under (`FIRECRAWL_PLAN_SCRAPES_PER_MINUTE` minus one). The crawler's own scrapes still share the plan's minute outside the bucket; a read they push over defers a minute.
  - **Before deferring, a read past the budget asks the shop's own page.** As decided: content there costs no credit and the read counts. Only a shop that cannot answer (error, timeout, redirect, shell) defers.
  - **A Firecrawl 429 is a deferral of one minute, not a counted or uncounted end.** The crawler shares the team's limit, so a read can find Firecrawl full with budget to spare. The shop's own page is tried first; if it cannot answer, the read reschedules after Firecrawl's minute and goes through the budget again, unless it already holds a reserved slot, which it keeps. `MAX_READ_DEFERRALS` (5) bounds a read's deferrals of either kind; past it the lot keeps its stamp and waits for its window, and a read at the cap reserves nothing. The Firecrawl component itself retries a 429 three times before surfacing it.
  - **The recommendation worker's deferred read throws like a failure.** The worker cannot wait for a slot; its hourly evidence retry covers the lot, as ADR-0008 set.
  - **`sentenceCandidates` splits on single newlines.** The reducer's block text separates blocks with one newline; the paragraph split (a blank line) that Firecrawl's markdown had satisfied left the whole page as one passage, so the worker's page evidence depended on markdown. One block per line reads both.
  - The markdown tolerance in the label strip (emphasis, table rows) stays for now; no source is markdown any more, and it can go with the next reader cleanup.
